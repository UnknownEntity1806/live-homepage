Draw & Chat 02: Halloween

October 28, 2026

In this second Draw & Chat I chat a little bit about my Halloween season this year whilst sharing the speedpaint of my Halloween illustration 🫣 .

🎃 [ transcript below ] 🎃

👉 You can view the finished illustration in the gallery here

👻 Can you believe that it is already nearing the end of October? 👻 I certainly cannot!I spent this month watching a lot of spooky movies and series, given that I could not really do a lot of other things due to my health.

It started with watching The OG Practical Magic, which I had never seen before. I really liked it and found it really funny that the typical fall music I hear all over TikTok right now was the soundtrack of that movie!

Since I really liked that one, of course I also had to watch the new sequel. I really liked it, although I thought it was a shame that Maisie Williams got so little airing time in the movie. But omg, I love the appearance of Lee Pace! I just recently watched the series featuring him in the Foundation and really liked it. Ofcourse, in my mind, he is always Thranduil first. But I liked it. Not as much as the first movie, but still.

I also watched the FEAR-trilogy movies on Netflix and really liked those. It had some nice twists in there and I really liked the way the story was dealt with moving back and forth in time.

Then I watched a spooky drama: East Palace, which was OK. AT some points I found the story somewhat confusing as I think not everything was explained well. But it was an entertaining watch.

Afterwards I watched the movie Backrooms. Which was a really nice movie, as it is somehow a portrayal of a feeling I often get in dreams. Yeah, it was weird, but worth the watch.

One that I kind of regret watching was Resident Evil: Welcome to Raccoon city. I think if you play the games it would be more entertaining. But it was not my cup of tea. Then I wanted to watch a classic, the SCREAM-series, but unfortunately I could not find the first one to watch on any of the services I have. So I settled for Nightmare on Elm Street. Which was kind of a disappointment. I felt that the story was lacking.

I finished my spooky season with the Netflix series Below. Which I really enjoyed. It was a fresh setting and a good watch.

So I made some spooky sketches whilst watching those and thought, why not turn one into an actual Halloween illustration for this year?

It has been quite some time since I felt inspired to draw, so this was a nice way to dip my toes in the drawing waters again.

I recorded the process and why not share it with you? I started with a photo of the sketch I made in my notebook and started working on it in Clip Studio Paint. At first I thought about drawing it with line-art. But as I started on that I realized that that would take away from the spooky feeling I wanted to give the illustration. So I changed my approach to that I call ‘digital painting’

I wanted the illustration to give that feeling of seeing shadows in a creepy dark forest. Perhaps intoxicated by the mushroom fumes.

I started working in a monochromatic purple colour scheme so that it would be more pleasant for me to look at while working on it. But later on I adjust the colors to achieve more of a horror effect.

I think it is funny that I managed to watch so many spooky things this year, as usually I get spooked really easily. But I handled them all okay this time around!

🎃 Some things I forgot to mention in the video: 🎃 I also watched the Carrie (2026) series and LOVED it! And I really want to watch all the Wednesday seasons next year and some more old classic horror movies.

Huggies, Malou
